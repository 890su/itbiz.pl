organisation's work points, then requested publication. The mark uses the
existing blue #3b82f6. The owner subsequently requested the previous lettering
with the new symbol. Both navigation variants now pair the symbol-only SVG

retain their localized accessible names in both page themes.

controls available at small widths. The SVG viewBox trims transparent
presentation padding without changing approved contours.
